FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does AppGap do?
AppGap analyzes your academics, activities, and awards and shows you where your college application profile is strong and where the real gaps are — with concrete direction on what to work on before you apply.
Is AppGap free to try?
Yes. You can build your profile and get started for free. Advanced features like the Personal Statement workspace and additional analyses are part of AppGap Pro.
Does AppGap guarantee admission or predict decisions?
No. AppGap gives you an honest read on where your current profile stands and realistic chances by school — it is not a guarantee of any admissions outcome, and it isn't a substitute for a professional counselor.
How are my chances calculated?
Chances come from a holistic model that weighs your academics, course rigor, activities, and awards against each school's selectivity — presented as an honest range, not a single inflated number.
Can AppGap help with my essays?
Yes. AppGap scores your activities and additional-info sections, and the Personal Statement and supplemental-essay workspaces give line-by-line coaching toward stronger drafts.
